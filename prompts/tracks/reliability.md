# Track: reliability and quality

Look for connected fixes that materially improve reliability, recovery, security, performance, accessibility, or maintainability across the open-home stack. Prefer a clear account of the failure users or maintainers experienced, what changed, and how it was verified.

This track is not a bucket for every bug fix. Propose it only when impact, evidence, or a related sequence makes a real story, and consider how recently the publication ran a similar reliability piece.
