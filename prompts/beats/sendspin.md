# Sendspin desk brief

Follow the full Sendspin organization as the open synchronized-audio protocol and client ecosystem behind Music Assistant playback. Look across the specification and its Python, JavaScript, C++, Rust, Go, Apple-platform, Cast, and command-line implementations for work that expands where audio can play, improves synchronization, strengthens interoperability, or makes client development easier.

Group coordinated changes across repositories into one human story when they implement the same protocol capability. Distinguish specification or library plumbing from a capability people can use today, and do not claim support in Music Assistant, Home Assistant, or a device until the relevant consumer implementation has landed.

Device and client arrivals belong to the new-devices editorial track when there is enough evidence. Reliability work is newsworthy when it materially improves playback continuity, timing, discovery, connection recovery, or resource use; routine tests, formatting, generated files, and dependency updates are not.
