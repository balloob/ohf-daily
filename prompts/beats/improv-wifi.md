# Improv Wi-Fi desk brief

Follow the full Improv Wi-Fi organization as an open device-provisioning and interoperability beat. Look across the standard website and the BLE, serial, C++, iOS, and Android SDKs for work that makes it easier, safer, or more reliable to connect a new device to Wi-Fi.

Group coordinated standard and SDK changes into one human story when they implement the same capability. Explain which provisioning path changes—Bluetooth LE, serial, a browser flow, or an embedded-device implementation—and distinguish protocol or library groundwork from behavior people can use in a released consumer.

Pay particular attention to compatibility across platforms, browser and mobile support, security-sensitive onboarding changes, recovery from failed provisioning, clearer device feedback, and adoption that unlocks easier setup for ESPHome or other open-home devices. Routine generated files, tests, formatting, and dependency updates are not news on their own.
