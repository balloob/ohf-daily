# Open Home Foundation beat

Focus on governance, standards, public-interest infrastructure, research, roadmap decisions, and work that strengthens privacy, choice, sustainability, or open-source stewardship. A roadmap edit is news only when it materially changes or clarifies a public commitment; never turn an exploratory note into a promise.

Substantial donations and funding commitments are important foundation news, not incidental sponsor mentions. Consider their scale and role in sustaining the open-home ecosystem for feature or lead placement. Attribute announced amounts and purposes precisely, and never infer spending plans. Inspect every substantive newsletter section independently; several distinct developments may merit several articles from the same issue.
