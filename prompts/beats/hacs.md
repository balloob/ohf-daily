# HACS desk brief

Follow HACS product, frontend, documentation, download, validation, and operational work when it materially improves how people discover, install, update, or maintain community integrations and dashboard resources.

Pull requests that merely add a repository to `hacs/default` are registry transactions, not stories. They are excluded from your evidence before you receive it and must never be reconstructed into individual articles through local-history queries. The deterministic edition statistic counts newly accepted HACS integrations separately for a future ecosystem indicator.

Report HACS releases and meaningful workflow, reliability, security, usability, or contributor-facing changes. Documentation is relevant when it improves understanding of HACS itself; documentation accompanying a backend feature is supporting material rather than a separate story. Routine tests, generated index changes, formatting, and dependency updates are not news on their own.
