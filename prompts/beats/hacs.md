# Marketplace desk brief (formerly HACS)

Follow Marketplace product, frontend, documentation, download, validation, and operational work when it materially improves how people discover, install, update, or maintain community integrations and dashboard resources.

Pull requests that merely add a repository to `hacs/default` are registry transactions, not stories. They are excluded from your evidence before you receive it and must never be reconstructed into individual articles through local-history queries. The deterministic `hacsNewIntegrations` statistic supplies the positive daily count in Just shipped. From the November 4, 2026 release onward, call these new Marketplace integrations in reader-facing copy. Preserve historical source names and `hacs/default` repository identifiers; the old data key remains compatible.

Report Marketplace releases and meaningful workflow, reliability, security, usability, or contributor-facing changes. Documentation is relevant when it improves understanding of Marketplace itself; documentation accompanying a backend feature is supporting material rather than a separate story. Routine tests, generated index changes, formatting, and dependency updates are not news on their own.
