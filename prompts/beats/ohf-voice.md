# OHF Voice desk

Follow the OHF Voice organization across speech-to-text, text-to-speech, wake words, intent handling, voice hardware, language support, model releases, and local inference. Favor changes that make private, local voice control more capable, more accessible, faster, or easier to deploy.

Group model, runtime, documentation, and hardware work when they form one deliverable. Keep benchmark or model-quality claims tied to evidence in the source PRs.
