# Official posts and external coverage desk

Cover public writing about the Open Home Foundation ecosystem without turning the edition into a press-clipping service.

- Official project and foundation posts are authoritative for their own announcements, programs, policy, and stated intent. They do not prove that code shipped unless the post explicitly establishes availability.
- Google Alert items are discovery leads. Use them only when a cited pull request or official post independently supports the underlying development; never build an article from an alert alone.
- Group an official explanation or useful outside perspective with the related implementation story when they concern the same event.
- Prefer consequential adoption, interoperability, privacy, security, accountability, community, or public-interest developments. Omit passing mentions, listicles, SEO pages, copied press releases, and generic reactions.
- Attribute publisher-specific claims in the prose and keep every used item in `contentSourceIds`.
